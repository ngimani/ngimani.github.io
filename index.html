What I Bring to a Procurement Team

The full purchase cycle

I work every step of a purchase myself: requisition, quotes, PO, receipt, invoice match and spend report. I know where things go wrong between those steps.

Volume without losing accuracy

Around 1,000 POs a month leaves no room for loose records. I check invoices against the PO and delivery note before anything is paid.

Stock that matches the system

Fresh produce spoils, so stock counts, variance checks and first-in-first-out rotation are part of my daily routine across two sites.

ERP from the support desk

As first-line NetSuite support I fix problems for other teams, write the SOPs and train users. I understand the system beyond data entry.

Built tools the team uses

Led a load control system and packhouse workbench for both sites, and set up a daily estimate report that emails customers in PDF and Excel.

Two languages, two regions

French native, English fluent, with work experience in Central Africa and the GCC. Comfortable with suppliers and colleagues from both.
